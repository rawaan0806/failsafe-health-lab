Explainability & limitations

09 / AUDIT
How does the optimizer work?Exact search evaluates 8 intervention combinations × 9 possible staff additions (up to 72 combined options). It maximizes an explicit weighted score under fictional budget constraints. This is mathematical optimization, not trained machine learning.
How robust is this result?Repeated simulations compare independent generated cohorts, paired across interventions.
Subgroup explorationSubgroup differences are descriptive and synthetic, not a clinical fairness audit.
Document this experiment
Research integrity: Every synthetic characteristic, probability, cost, and intervention effect is an illustrative assumption. This model does not forecast clinical outcomes, establish healthcare effectiveness, estimate actual hospital costs, or recommend treatment. The model has no live medical records and is not for patient-care decisions. Real application would require literature-based calibration, calendar-aware capacity modeling, expert review, bias testing and external clinical validation.
